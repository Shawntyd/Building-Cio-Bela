We're not starting from scratch.

Become a founding stylist

No existing format to fit into. We're building it together.

At most established salons, you join a brand that's already decided and learn to fit its rules. Here, the brand identity is still being written, and we're writing it as a team. Founding stylists help decide how the space looks, how it works, and what Cio Bela stands for, and then get to watch it come to life around them. Years from now, when clients walk into the finished salon, you'll know you helped build something bigger than any one of us.

A multi-service ecosystem

Your neighbors here send you clients. They don't compete for them.

In a hair-only salon, every stylist is chasing the same clients, and nobody hands their regulars to the chair next door. Cio Bela works differently. You're surrounded by artists in other specialties who see clients every day who need a stylist, and none of them do hair.

Each lash fill, wax, manicure, and tanning session is a client already in the building, already trusting the brand, and one recommendation away from your chair. And your clients discover the rest of the salon too, which keeps them coming back to all of us.
